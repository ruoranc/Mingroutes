# China Historical Transportation GIS Project (CHTGIS)

## Official Courier Network in Late Imperial China

### [English Version](https://ruoranc.github.io/Mingroutes/index_en.html)

The **China Historical Transportation GIS Project (CHTGIS)** is a long-term project that reconstructs historical transport networks in China and models changes in travel time, accessibility, and transport costs over time.

The first phase of the project, completed in 2020, reconstructs the **official courier network of the Ming dynasty (1368–1644)**. I geocoded historical courier stations and reconstructed the routes connecting them from historical records. The reconstructed network was subsequently refined using digital elevation models (DEMs) to account for terrain constraints and plausible historical travel corridors.

This Ming courier network forms the first historical layer of a broader GIS framework designed to study the evolution of transportation, urban systems, and economic geography in China between the fourteenth and eighteenth centuries.

If you use this dataset, please cite:

**Cheng, Ruoran. “Transportation Cost in the Great Divergence: Yangtze China vs. England.” LSE Economic History Working Paper No. 371, 2024.**
[PDF](https://www.lse.ac.uk/Economic-History/Assets/Documents/WorkingPapers/Economic-History/2024/WP371.pdf)

I received my PhD in Economic History from the **London School of Economics and Political Science (LSE)**. My research focuses on the historical geography of cities and transportation, particularly the long-run evolution of transport networks, accessibility, and urban systems.

For more information about my research, please visit my [personal website](https://ruoranc.github.io/website/).

## 中文版

### [中文页面](https://ruoranc.github.io/Mingroutes/index.html)

**中国历史交通 GIS 项目（China Historical Transportation GIS Project, CHTGIS）** 是一个长期研究项目，旨在重建中国历史时期的交通网络，并据此刻画交通时间、空间可达性与运输成本的长期变化。

项目第一阶段于 2020 年完成，主要重建**明代（1368–1644）的官方驿传网络**。我根据历史文献对明代驿站进行地理编码，并重建连接各驿站的交通路线。在此基础上，进一步结合数字高程模型（DEM）对路线进行地形约束与优化，使重建的交通网络更接近历史时期可能实际使用的交通廊道。

明代驿传网络构成 CHTGIS 的第一个历史交通网络图层。整个项目的长期目标，是建立一个覆盖中国十四至十八世纪历史交通体系的 GIS 框架，用于研究交通网络、空间可达性、城市体系与经济地理的长期演化。

如使用本数据集，请引用：

**Cheng, Ruoran. “Transportation Cost in the Great Divergence: Yangtze China vs. England.” LSE Economic History Working Paper No. 371, 2024.**
[PDF](https://www.lse.ac.uk/Economic-History/Assets/Documents/WorkingPapers/Economic-History/2024/WP371.pdf)
